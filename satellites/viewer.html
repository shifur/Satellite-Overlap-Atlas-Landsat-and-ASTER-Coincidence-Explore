How to interpret this atlas

The dashboard identifies potential satellite overlaps based on orbit timing and location. It is a geometric screening product, not an acquisition catalog.

  • A close modeled pass does not guarantee that both instruments collected usable imagery.
  • Cloud cover, instrument scheduling, data quality and full-footprint overlap must be verified separately.

Use the atlas to find promising space-time windows, then check authoritative mission archives for actual imagery.
